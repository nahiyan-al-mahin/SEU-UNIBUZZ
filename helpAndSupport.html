Welcome to UniBuzz

A campus marketplace built by students, for students. Buy, sell, fund ideas, and grow together — all within Southeast University.

What is UniBuzz?
A little background on why we built this
Campus Marketplace
Students can buy and sell products directly within Southeast University. From handmade crafts to digital goods — if a student made it, it belongs here. No middlemen, no hassle.
Ideas Hub
Got a startup idea? Post it. Want to back a classmate's dream? Fund it. UniBuzz gives student entrepreneurs a real platform to pitch, get funded, and grow their business.
Community First
We built UniBuzz because we saw talented students with no place to showcase their work. This platform keeps money and opportunity inside our own campus community.
Built at SEU
UniBuzz is a university project developed as part of our coursework at Southeast University, Tejgaon, Dhaka. Every line of code was written by students who actually use it.
